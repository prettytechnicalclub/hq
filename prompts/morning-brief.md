# Morning brief prompt (scheduled task)

Set this up as a daily scheduled task in Claude (e.g. 6:45am your time) with Google Calendar,
Gmail, and your dashboard connected. It writes a brief your dashboard shows at the top.

---

Write today's morning brief. Do not send emails, accept or change calendar events, or change
anything except the brief.

1. **Calendar:** today's events across my calendars except [shared family calendar name] and
   holidays. Skip events I declined and invites I haven't accepted.
2. **Email:** inbox from the last 2 days, excluding promotions, social and forums. Only what
   needs a reply or action from me (partners, clients, investors, bills, deadlines).
3. **Dashboard:** read my open to-dos, upcoming beauty appointments and dated events.
4. **Write the brief** as:
   - **Headline:** one sentence on what kind of day it is and the one thing that matters most.
   - **Today:** time · event.
   - **Needs a reply:** who — what, one line each.
   - **Focus:** the 3 to-dos most worth doing today, with the project in brackets.
   - **Coming up:** the next 7 days, anything worth prepping for.
   Max 5 items per section, short plain lines, no emoji.

If a connector isn't reachable, write what you can and say what was missing.
