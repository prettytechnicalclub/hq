# Build your own HQ (prompt)

Use this if you'd rather have Claude build an HQ from scratch around your life instead of
editing this repo's `index.html`. Paste into Claude with artifacts on and Google Calendar +
Gmail connected. Fill in the brackets first.

---

Build me **HQ**, a one-page personal operating system I open every morning. Dark, translucent
"liquid glass" panels, soft drifting color, one accent color per area, readable on my phone.
Save everything I enter.

**The home page, top to bottom**
1. **Today timeline.** My morning routine first, as ONE row I can tick in a tap, or open to tick
   each step ([your steps, e.g. make bed, morning sunlight, vitamins, stretches, skincare]).
   Then emails and messages I need to reply to today. Then my Google Calendar events in time
   order (all my calendars except [any to hide]; skip declined events and invites I haven't
   accepted), then my evening routine. A "Now" line. Arrows to see other days.
2. **Morning brief** (written each morning by a scheduled task, see `morning-brief.md`).
3. **Priorities:** today's top 3, checkable. **Quick notes:** a box that saves as I type.
4. **Action items:** every open to-do from my areas, tagged with where it lives.
5. **From your email:** to-dos Claude pulls from my inbox (no promos or newsletters), each
   with Open email / Add as action item / Done.
6. **This week:** a 7-day calendar view.

**Areas (tabs across the top)**
- **Work:** [Ventures: a card per company, client or project with status, key facts, links
  and its own to-dos], [Money: monthly subscriptions with totals, monthly income / spending /
  saving targets, money goals].
- **Life:** [Plans: cards for trips, moves, big projects], [Diary], [Notes], [Vision Board],
  [Routines & Beauty], [Social Life: events that show on my timeline], [Growth & Learning].

Only build the areas I listed. Keep copy short and plain.
